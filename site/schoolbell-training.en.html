Training · Shkolnik

Practice with a training plan

Practice after initial setup. Start in the demo: data stays in this browser. On a device use separate training plans and back up existing data first. These exercises do not change clocks or NTP or enable automatic bells. Example dates are not the current date.

UI version: {{VERSION}}. This is training, not a hardware acceptance record. Steps are manual; this page changes nothing.

1. Create a schedule2. Copy and discard an edit3. Paint dates across a year boundary4. Preview the selected day5. Listen in the browser6. Export .sbp and inspect its contents7. Keep a backup and event log8. Finish training

Demo and device

1. Create a schedule

  1. Open «Расписания» (Schedules), choose Create, name it “Training — school day” and choose a color.
  2. Add an enabled 09:00 row with the bundled test bell. Leave the pre-bell off.
  3. Save the draft, wait for confirmation, reload and select the schedule again.

Expected result: Name, color, time and melody persist; automatic bells remain off.

Troubleshooting / important behavior

If saving is unconfirmed, keep the tab and edits open; use «Решить проблему» (Troubleshoot).

Demo and device

2. Copy and discard an edit

  1. Copy the training schedule as “Training — copy”, change its time to 09:05 and save.
  2. Check that the original still says 09:00.
  3. Change the copy to 09:10 without saving, then choose Discard edits / load saved and confirm.

Expected result: The copy returns to 09:05; the original is unchanged.

Troubleshooting / important behavior

Discard applies to all unsaved draft edits. Do this exercise only when there are no other changes to keep.

Demo and device

3. Paint dates across a year boundary

  1. Create “Training — year boundary” in Calendar, from 31 December 2030 through 2 January 2031 inclusive; apply the name and dates.
  2. If another plan occupies these dates, choose a free year boundary. Plan ranges cannot overlap.
  3. Select the original training schedule color, paint only 1 January, switch to View day, save and reload.

Expected result: December and January appear together; only 1 January is assigned.

Troubleshooting / important behavior

View day does not erase assignments. Quiet day / eraser removes them. Long plans use pagination beside the months.

Demo and device

4. Preview the selected day

  1. In Settings select «По календарному плану» (By calendar plan) and save the draft. Do not apply or enable it.
  2. In Calendar select View day and click the painted 1 January. Check the preview date and click Show bells if needed.
  3. Also preview 31 December and 2 January.

Expected result: 1 January has one 09:00 bell; adjacent dates have none.

Troubleshooting / important behavior

If a painted date is quiet, check the draft mode and whether the schedule row is enabled. Painting does not change the mode. Preview does not validate system time or readiness to run.

Demo and device · sound only on your click

5. Listen in the browser

  1. Lower your computer or phone volume.
  2. Click 🔊 in the test-bell schedule row (Play in browser).

Expected result: The computer or phone plays a short bell; automation stays off.

Troubleshooting / important behavior

Check browser volume and melody if silent. 🔔 uses Shkolnik speakers and is unavailable in the demo.

Pages · bundled melodies

6. Export .sbp and inspect its contents

  1. Open Calendar → Portable calendar plans .sbp; explicitly choose the time zone and training plan.
  2. Download selected plans. Apply any pending plan name/date edits first.
  3. Select the downloaded file for preview, inspect proposed names and choose Check addition without writing.

Expected result: The preview lists contents and conflicts. Re-importing into the same data can report overlapping dates; that is expected.

Troubleshooting / important behavior

Pages only previews imports; it does not add them to its draft. A compatible device can add plans and dependencies without overwriting existing data. Resolve conflicts first; importing does not enable bells.

Installed module only; explore the log in the demo

7. Keep a backup and event log

  1. On the device open Settings → Backup and download .sbb. Check that it was saved; do not restore it in this exercise.
  2. In the Journal try pagination and Errors, then Copy and paste into a text editor.

Expected result: .sbb backs up the module; .sbp transfers selected plans and dependencies. Copy includes all retained history, not just the filtered page.

Troubleshooting / important behavior

Downloading alone does not verify restoration. Test restoration separately after backing up current data; automatic bells remain off after restore.

Demo and device

8. Finish training

  1. Check that automatic bells remain off. Keep the training draft, or delete only training plans, then unused training schedules, and save.
  2. Do not delete other users’ data or reset a demo containing wanted plans.
  3. Test speakers, activation and restoration separately once time, volume and a backup are ready.

Expected result: You can create, save, inspect and transfer a plan without starting automation.

Troubleshooting / important behavior

Sound mute and automation stop are different actions. Draft and applied plan are separate copies; the header starts the previously applied plan. Confirming time does not set the clock.
